### 3. Scripting & TOC UI Bug Fixes
- **The Problem:** The Table of Contents failed for complex articles using raw HTML `<h2>` tags (since Astro only generated IDs for Markdown `##` headings).
- **The Fix:** Injected a client-side JS block in `src/layouts/PostLayout.astro` that scans the DOM on load, identifies `<h2>`/`<h3>` tags without an `id`, automatically generates a URL-friendly slug, and dynamically injects the ID. The TOC now tracks scroll position flawlessly across all formats.
